# ScrollMi: The Science of the Scroll — speaker notes

Notes for each slide of [ScrollMi-deck.pdf](ScrollMi-deck.pdf), in order. Numbers and sources are in [data/TAGGING.md](../data/TAGGING.md).

## Why a scroll is personality data

### 1. ScrollMi

ScrollMi turns a scroll into a personality read. Four letter axes plus a separate gender lean, every weight tied to a study.

### 2. 93%

The premise: what you like online is personality data. The 2013 Facebook study predicted gender at 93% from likes alone. ScrollMi does the same thing in the open, with reels.

## How each clip is weighed

### 3. How a scroll becomes a type

The feed on the left is the real app. Every tap is weighed twice: by what the clip signals, and by how long you watched before deciding. The panel updates after every reaction.

### 4. The intended look

The intended aesthetic and branding for ScrollMi: analogue paper meets a digital feed. Receipts, ID cards and playlists as the visual language, sage green and black ink, a red pen for anything personal. This deck is styled the same way.

### 5. Every weight is capped by its evidence

Weights run from minus one to plus one. The ceiling for any weight depends on its evidence tier, so strong claims need strong studies. That is what keeps this from being vibes.

### 6. Each clip has a fingerprint

Four real clips. The Kafka quote is almost pure Intuition. The bungee jump is thrill-seeking: Perceiving, Extraverted, guy-leaning. Cleaning is the strongest Judging clip and girl-leaning. The social battery meme only says one thing: Introvert.

### 7. Same tap, different signals

A like on the money clip says organized, practical, a little Thinking. Deep hugs is Feeling. The dance circle is the most extraverted clip. The makeup clip barely moves personality but is the strongest gender signal at plus point nine.

## The research behind the weights

### 8. What your taste says about your traits

Three findings that set weights. Openness predicts art taste in about 91,000 people, the biggest single study here. Activity tracks extraversion and conscientiousness, which is why gym clips carry small Extraverted and Judging weights. The mapping from letters to Big Five traits is McCrae and Costa, 1989.

### 9. Who watches what

Gender is tracked as its own bar, never part of the four letters. Beauty content is the clearest signal, so it gets the top weight. Cleaning audiences run 82 to 96 percent female, but that is creator analytics, so it stops at point six. Thrill-seeking is higher in men by about half a standard deviation.

## Watch time as a signal

### 10. Fast decisions mean strong preferences

Decision science: response time peaks when you are indifferent and falls as preference strengthens, in either direction. So a snap like or a snap dislike is strong evidence. That is the left edge of ScrollMi's curve.

### 11. Likes are a U. Dislikes fall.

This is ScrollMi's actual weight function. Likes are U-shaped: strong when instant, weakest mid-clip, strongest after a rewatch. Dislikes are strongest when instant and weakest after a full watch, where it could be hate-watching.

### 12. Each point on the curve, and its evidence

Every point on the curve has a source and a tier. The fast-decision and finish-watching rules rest on peer-reviewed work. Rewatch and long-watch dislikes rest on weaker evidence, so they move the weight less and level off.

## Results, curation and limits

### 13. Does watch time help?

Fake users with known personalities take the quiz. With watch-time weights, error drops about five percent and correlation rises from 0.77 to 0.81. This depends on assumed viewing behavior, so real playtests are the next check. On the real catalog every user sees every clip, landing around 66 to 70 percent of letters right.

### 14. 45 clips in, 27 kept

We cut 18 of 45 clips: every animal clip, three touchy topics, and ten weak or duplicate signals. The chart shows how many strong clips each pole keeps. Thinking is the gap: one strong clip.

### 15. What we don’t know yet

Three honest gaps. Thinking has one strong clip. Feeling dropped to three when the animals went. And no real people have been measured yet. The results page already asks people their known type, which is how we will measure real accuracy.
